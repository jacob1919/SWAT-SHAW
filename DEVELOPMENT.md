## Validation constraints

Use identical input bytes in the three runs. Preserve the existing numeric hydrologic group correction (3 -> C). Daily diagnostics may be enabled identically. The supplied archive has no accompanying observed discharge/soil-temperature validation series; comparisons are numerical and process diagnostics, not claims of accuracy. Humidity, wind and solar radiation are generated by SWAT+'s weather generator. The old FT checkout includes changes outside soil physics, so its difference from official is not a clean attribution experiment.
